"Password Expiry" Credential Phishing

Critical Severity
From: Microsoft Security Team <no-reply@ms-verify.org> SUSPICIOUS
Subject: ACTION REQUIRED: Your password expires in 24 hours
To: you@yourcompany.com
■ Microsoft Account Security

Dear User,

Our security system has detected suspicious login attempts on your account from an unrecognized location (Moscow, Russia).

To prevent permanent account suspension, you must verify your identity immediately. Failure to do so within 24 hours will result in:

  • Permanent account lockout
  • Loss of all emails and files
  • Password reset via admin
Verify Your Account Now

This is an automated message. Please do not reply to this email.

Red Flags to Look For

!

Spoofed Sender Domain

The email comes from "ms-verify.org" — not an official Microsoft domain. Legitimate Microsoft emails always come from @microsoft.com or @accountprotection.microsoft.com.

!

Artificial Urgency

"24 hours or permanent lockout" is a pressure tactic. Attackers create panic to stop you from thinking critically. Real companies give generous notice periods.

!

Threats of Loss

Threatening loss of emails, files, and account access is designed to trigger an emotional response. Legitimate services never threaten total data loss.

!

Generic Greeting & Suspicious Link

"Dear User" instead of your name. And the "Verify" button would link to a fake login page designed to steal your credentials. Always hover over links before clicking.

!

Poor Grammar / Unprofessional Tone

Legitimate companies use professional, measured language. Threats of "permanent suspension" and "loss of all files" are not how real support teams communicate.
