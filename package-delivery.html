"Customs Fee" Package Delivery Scam

Medium Severity
From: FedEx Delivery <noreply@fedex-track.com> SUSPICIOUS
Subject: Your package #FDX9281 is held at customs
To: you@yourcompany.com
📦 FedEx

Dear Customer,

Your shipment #FDX9281 has arrived at the international sorting facility but is currently held at customs due to unpaid import duties.

A small customs processing fee of $2.99 is required to release your package. The package contains:

  • 1x Electronic Device
  • Weight: 1.2 kg
  • Estimated delivery: Tomorrow by 5:00 PM
Pay Customs Fee ($2.99)

You will be redirected to our secure payment portal.

Red Flags to Look For

💳

Small Amount to Bypass Suspicion

$2.99 seems too small to be a scam — that's intentional. The real target is your credit card details and personal information entered on the fake payment page.

🌐

Spoofed Domain

"fedex-track.com" is not an official FedEx domain (which is fedex.com). Tracking links from delivery companies should always use their main domain.

📦

Vague Package Details

"1x Electronic Device" with no brand, no model, no order number. Real delivery notifications include specific tracking numbers you can verify on the official site.

🚨

Were You Expecting a Package?

If you haven't ordered anything, you won't have customs fees. These scams are sent in bulk hoping to catch people who happen to be expecting a delivery.

🔍

Always Check Tracking Independently

Go directly to fedex.com and enter any tracking number manually. Never click links in unsolicited delivery emails — they lead to fake login/payment pages.
