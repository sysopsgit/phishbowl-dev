"Overdue Invoice" Financial Fraud

Critical Severity
From: Accounts Payable <invoices@quickbooks-billing.com> SUSPICIOUS
Subject: Overdue Invoice #8842 - Payment Required
To: you@yourcompany.com
■ QuickBooks Billing

Dear Valued Customer,

Our records indicate that Invoice #8842 for $3,450.00 is now 15 days past due.

Please find the invoice attached. Payment must be received within 48 hours to avoid the following:

  • Service suspension
  • Late payment penalty of $150
  • Referral to collections agency
View & Pay Invoice

Red Flags to Look For

!

Suspicious Attachment (ZIP file)

Legitimate invoices are PDFs, not ZIP files. ZIP attachments from unknown senders almost always contain malware. Never open them.

!

Pressure for Immediate Payment

Threatening service suspension and collections within 48 hours is a classic pressure tactic. Always verify invoices through your internal accounting system.

!

Fake QuickBooks Domain

"quickbooks-billing.com" is not owned by Intuit. The real domain is quickbooks.intuit.com. Always check the sender's domain carefully.

!

No Specific Details

No company name, no product details, no dates. A real invoice references specific services rendered and specific dates of service.

!

Unverified Sender

If you don't recognize an invoice, contact your finance team directly (don't reply to the email). Never pay an unsolicited invoice.
